SKROL is a premium short-form content tracking app helping users reduce doomscrolling and understand their scrolling behavior.

Track every Instagram Reel and YouTube Short you watch. See real screen time from UsageStats — not estimates. Get a daily attention score, weekly insights, and smart recommendations to scroll less and live more.

FEATURES
• Live Reels & Shorts counter with on-screen overlay
• Real social media screen time per app
• Attention score and daily insights
• Focus mode and habit tracking
• Weekly reports and activity heatmap
• Pink brain mascot — your SKROL companion

SKROL stores all data locally on your device. No account required. No ads.
