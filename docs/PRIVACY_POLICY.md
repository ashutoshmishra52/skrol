# Privacy Policy for SKROL

**Last Updated:** June 6, 2026

## Introduction

SKROL ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed by Ashutosh Mishra. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect any personal information.**

SKROL operates entirely offline. All data is stored locally on your device.

## Data Stored Locally

The following data is stored on your device only:
- App usage statistics
- Focus session history
- Habit tracking data
- User preferences and settings
- Gamification progress (XP, achievements)

## Permissions

### Usage Stats (Required)
Used to monitor app usage and provide wellness insights. Data never leaves your device.

### Notifications (Required)
Used for focus reminders, streak alerts, and intervention prompts.

### Accessibility Service (Optional)
Used only for Monk Mode to help block distracting apps during focus sessions.

## Third-Party Services

SKROL does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit any user data to third parties.

## Data Export

You can export your data at any time through Settings > Export Data.

## Children's Privacy

SKROL does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy. Changes will be reflected in the app settings.
